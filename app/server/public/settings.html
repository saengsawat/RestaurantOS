Settings

This is where the restaurant stops being a demo. The name in every header, the address on every receipt, and the people who can sign in are all data on the server, so all of it is yours to set. Every change here is manager work: the PIN is asked once and checked again on the server for each one.

Timezone

The business day still buckets on the server's own clock, so changing this does not move which day a late check lands on yet. That wiring is its own ticket.

Pay period
Cycle starts

Any past start date works; the cycles count forward and backward from it. Twice a month needs no anchor: it runs the 1st to the 15th, then the 16th to the end of the month.

Team

A job title is what the room calls somebody; the role chip beside it is what they may do. They are separate on purpose, so giving the dishwasher a better title never hands them the void button.

Phone, email, and emergency contact are manager-only. They are fetched under a manager PIN that is checked again on every read, and they never travel with the roster the rest of the app uses. No wage, tax, or bank detail is kept here at all: that belongs to a payroll provider, not to us.

Deactivating is soft and always will be: their PIN stops opening anything, and every check they ever opened still carries their name. PINs are stored hashed and are never shown here, not even to a manager, because the only honest answer to a forgotten PIN is a new one.
