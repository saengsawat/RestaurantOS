Reservations
Reading the book...

A reservation is a promise, not a lock. The floor badges a booking as its hour comes round, and seating somebody else there warns you once and then does what you asked. Nothing here ever refuses a seating.

Past-due bookings stay in the book until somebody marks them, because a reservation that quietly disappears at 7:31 is how a no-show goes unrecorded. No-show and cancel are both states, never deletions: the row keeps its place and the audit trail keeps the actor.
